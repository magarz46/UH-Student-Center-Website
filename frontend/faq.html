Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Student Center hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM

Where is the UH Sugar Land Student Center located?

The UH Sugar Land Student Center is located at 13850 University Blvd, Sugar Land, TX 77479 on the first floor of Sugar Land Academic Building 1 (SAB1)

What services does the Student Center offer?

The Student Center offers study spaces, student connection via our common area, and access to exclusive seminars and events each semester!

How do I book a study room?

Study rooms can be booked directly on the UH Sugar Land Student Center website. Simply navigate to the "Room Booking" page and complete the form. If the space is available, your request will be accepted!

I am having trouble using the website, who can I contact?

Please visit the "Contact" page to get in contact with members of our staff. We would be pleased to assist you with any issues you encounter.
